# Zone Lite 2.6.14 SLICTOWER 3FL delivery-fault HIL patch

The signed 2.6.13 HIL image fixed the G3 live-frame parser. SLICTOWER 3FL has delivered fresh `LIVE` punches to ADD and Oracle, and its storage recovery and persistence checks passed. During an ADD backend deployment, a failed ADD outbox enqueue latched the ESP's `LOCAL_FAILURE` state even though the primary attendance record was durably preserved. The device reported `ESP_DURABILITY_FAULT` until an ESP-only power cycle, after which ADD showed ONLINE, healthy storage, and zero read/write failures. A later live punch waited two seconds for the local outbox lock, received a direct ADD acknowledgement, and again latched `LOCAL_FAILURE` despite healthy flash diagnostics. This second event shows that delivery recovery alone did not clear the HIL fault.

2.6.14 keeps the 2.6.13 attendance parser and stops classifying the preserved attendance delivery retry as a flash failure. When a healthy storage lock is temporarily unavailable, the live punch can instead use an acknowledged ADD fallback without a permanent storage fault; it emits `LIVE_OUTBOX_LOCK_RECOVERED` for monitoring. A missing lock, unacknowledged fallback, or actual queue admission, write, read, or persistence failure still latches a local fault. The release admits the exact signed 2.6.13 application digest as a qualified predecessor and retains the prior signed baseline list. It remains `HIL_ONLY` for the same five exact Swat, SLICTOWER, and Peshawar targets.

Trial SLICTOWER 3FL first. Require signed OTA boot, healthy storage and workers, an accepted heartbeat, a fresh biometric `LIVE` punch acknowledged by Oracle, and physical rejection of card/PIN punch methods where a test user is available. Watch for repeated outbox lock recoveries; a healthy LED alone would not prove that contention is resolved. Do not treat a source-recovered or blocked-identity event as live HIL evidence. Keep SLICTOWER 13FL off further OTA trials while its flash-full blocked-attendance queue is preserved and diagnosed.
